Class: SequenceTranslation

SequenceTranslation

SequenceTranslation draws all six reading frames around the sequence when enough base-pair detail is visible. Direct frames are drawn outside the backbone and reverse frames are drawn inside it. Reading frames restart at each contig boundary. Protein sequences are not stored: codons intersecting the visible range are translated and drawn in a streaming pass. Lane size and opacity increase smoothly with zoom rather than switching on at a cutoff.

SequenceTranslation is configured through Sequence#translation.
